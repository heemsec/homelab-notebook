Homelab Notebook Privacy policy

PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026. This covers the website and a packaged Android app, if you install one.

No account, and no server

There's no sign-in. No analytics either. Homelab Notebook doesn't keep a backend for your lab. It's a page in your browser, and it doesn't upload your hosts, IPs, or notes.

What stays on this device

Hosts, services, and the theme sit in local storage on this device. Clear the site data and they're gone. Export JSON or YAML writes a file on your device; import reads a file you choose. Nothing in those files is sent to this site.

Install the app and a service worker keeps a copy of the pages so it can open offline. That's the app itself, not your notebook being sent anywhere.

Questions

If something here is unclear, open an issue on the GitHub issues page for this repo.
